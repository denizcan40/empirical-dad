Evidence-based parenting for dads who don't plan to slow down. A daily briefing from the first positive test to age three, numbered by the day of pregnancy the way your OB counts it. Every claim is graded out loud as established, strong or emerging, and every risk gets a real number. One angle a day (body, brain, physiology, parents, adventure, protocol, myths) and one small action at the end. A personal research briefing, not medical advice.
